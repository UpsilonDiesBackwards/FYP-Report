\chapter{Understanding Paper Approachability}

Findings across multiple questionnaire sections suggest that there is a fundamental disconnect in scientific literature with game development.
While the survey participants showed high levels of curiosity and intrigue, this interest is also being constrained by the daunting nature of the academia. \\

The data suggests a contradiction. Developers are constantly looking for knowledge to do what they need, yet the lack the
immediate tools and requisites to process it. This is evidenced through the high reliance on external sources {(\textbf{43\%} look up definitions)}
alongside the noticeable percentage that skips past the mathematics to look for code snippets \textbf{(14\%)}.
The current academic environment requires the developer to become an amateur mathematician or physicist to in order to start programming. \\

The contrast between professional journals \textbf{(57\% Confusing/Dense)} and blogs \\ \textbf{(91\% Clear/Understandable)} indicates two different educational ecosystems.
Where academic papers are built primarily for specialist-to-specialist communication, whilst developers seem to prefer a less rigorous communicative approach.
This suggests that formal literature fails at its primary purpose of clear dissemination of information. \\

The reluctance to translate notation in to code highlights a core barrier. Theoretical concepts are presented in an abstract, formal manner that does not map
well into practical logic. The mathematics is taught as a concept (\textit{what}) rather than as a process (\textit{how})

\section{Case Studies}

To illustrate the inherent challenges in current academic literature, a selection of papers were analysed.

\subsection{\bookcite{koschierSurveySPHMethods2022}{}}

The structure of this paper arguably severely limits the comprehension when looking at it from an approachability view.

When scoring the paper against our four \textbf{Accessibility Dimensions} it scored:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Notation:} The text heavily relies on specialised abstract mathematical notation which are never properly defined for a non-specialist, scoring \textbf{0/4}
    \item \textbf{Clarity of Lexicon:} The jargon used (e.g., `Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics', and `Lagrangian frame') assumes that the reader has an existing advanced knowledge of the content, scoring \textbf{1/4}
    \item \textbf{Contexualisation:} While diagrams exist, they are mathematically complex and do not provide functional code examples, instead offering highly-abstracted pseudocode, scoring \textbf{2/4}
    \item \textbf{Pedagogy:} The paper functions as a survey \textit{for specialists} rather than a teaching tool. It assumes existing foundational knowledge meaning that it does not intend to teach the reader, scoring \textbf{0/4}
\end{enumerate}

The resulting \textbf{Approachability Score} (AS) for this type of literature is therefore:

\begin{equation}
    \text{AS} = \frac{0 + 1 + 2 + 0}{4} = 0.75 / 4
\end{equation}

This very low score reinforces the preliminary findings that, while valuable to researchers, the paper functions as a profound barrier for the average game developer

\subsection{\bookcite{LearnOpenGLExtensive}{}}

When scoring this blog against the \textbf{Accessibility Dimensions} it scored:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Notation:} Concepts likes matrices, vectors, etc., are never presented purely abstractly. They are shown being \textbf{used} within a function signature or variable declaration in code (e.g., \mintinline{c++}|mat3  modelMatrix|), it scores \textbf{3/4}
    \item \textbf{Clarity of Lexicon:} The language, whilst technical, is often framed in functional terms. Instead of purely abstracted mathematical jargon, it uses terminology such as `View Matrix', `Projection Pipeline', it scores \textbf{3/4}
    \item \textbf{Contextualisation:} Every concept has:
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item An explanation of the theoretics (\textit{what it is})
            \item Diagrams showing data or other information
            \item Fully functional, commented code snippets that implement the theory discussed
        \end{enumerate}
        it scores \textbf{4/4}
    \item \textbf{Pedagogy:} Due to it being \textit{focused} on being a learning resource, it excels in attempting to \textit{teach} the reader
    the concepts discussed. It structures the teaching resources so that the reader incrementally learns new information, whilst still being able to understand the context
    of the text. It scores \textbf{3/4}
\end{enumerate}

The resulting \textbf{Approachability Score} (AS) for this type of literature is therefore:

\begin{equation}
    \text{AS} = \frac{3 + 3 + 4 + 3}{4} = 3.25 / 4
\end{equation}

This is a very high score, and is a great learning resource for new and returning developers. It remains evident why it is considered the go-to resource for
OpenGL graphics programming.
